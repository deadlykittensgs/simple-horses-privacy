# Privacy Policy for Simple Horses

**Effective Date:** April 3, 2026

## Overview

Simple Horses ("the App") is a horse marketplace and classifieds platform. This privacy policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a user.

---

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Account information:** First name, last name, email address, username, phone number (optional), and city/location when you register.
- **Profile information:** Bio, profile photo, social media handles (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, website) that you choose to add.
- **Listings:** Photos, descriptions, prices, and location information for items you post for sale.
- **Messages:** Messages sent between users through the in-app messaging system.

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Usage data:** Which screens you visit and how you interact with the app.
- **Device information:** Device type and operating system version for compatibility purposes.

---

## How We Use Your Information

- To create and manage your account
- To display your listings and profile to other users
- To facilitate messaging between buyers and sellers
- To show your username and ratings on listings you post
- To send you notifications about messages and sales

---

## Information Shared With Other Users

The following information may be visible to other logged-in users of the app:
- Your username, first name, last name, and profile photo
- Your city/location (if set)
- Your bio and social media links (if set)
- Your phone number **only if you enable "Show phone on public profile"**
- Your seller ratings (thumbs up / thumbs down totals)
- Your active listings

---

## Data Storage

All data is stored securely using [Supabase](https://supabase.com), a cloud database provider. Data is stored on servers in the United States. Supabase's privacy policy applies to data storage: https://supabase.com/privacy

---

## Photos

Photos you upload to listings or your profile are stored in Supabase Storage and are publicly accessible via URL to any user of the app.

---

## Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may delete your account from the Profile screen, which will remove your account access. To request complete deletion of all your data, contact us at the email below.

---

## Children's Privacy

Simple Horses is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

---

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access the personal information we hold about you
- Update or correct your information (via the Profile screen)
- Delete your account (via the Profile screen)
- Request complete data deletion by contacting us
